understand your skin

Everyone's skin is different. Learning about your skin type can help you make better choices for your skincare routine.

dry skin

Dry skin may feel tight or rough and may benefit from gentle and hydrating skincare products.

oily skin

Oily skin can produce more natural oil and may benefit from lightweight skincare products.

combination skin

Combination skin can have both oily and dry areas, making balance an important part of skincare.

sensitive skin

Sensitive skin may react more easily to certain ingredients, so gentle products can be helpful.
